How to install

Your journey, on your Home Screen.

Add My GOAT Journey to your Home Screen and open it like an app. Keep tracking your habits offline after initial setup.

iPhone · Safari

  1. Open the app in Safari using the link below.
  2. Choose Share → Add to Home Screen. You may need to open Safari’s More menu to find Share.
  3. Leave Open as Web App enabled if offered, then tap Add.

Android · Chrome

  1. Open the app in Chrome using the link below.
  2. Open the browser menu (⋮) → Install and create shortcut → Install. Wording varies: you may see Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions and confirm installation.

Computer · Supported browsers

  1. Open the app in a browser that supports installation, such as Chrome.
  2. Use its install control or menu. In Chrome: Cast, save, and share → Install page as app…
  3. Confirm installation. If unavailable, keep using the app in your browser or bookmark it.

A little less online. A little more yours.

Your routine is personal. Your data is too.

No account, login, or email address required. Your habit definitions, completion records, and progress history are stored in the browser on your device.

These records are not automatically sent to a backend or synchronized across devices. You decide when to export a backup and where to keep it.

Local also means yours to look after: clearing browser or site data can remove your records. Keep regular, private backups.

A few things worth knowing

Are my habit records sent to a server?

No. The app stores your habit records in this browser’s local database, not on an application server. There is no automatic cross-device synchronization.

How do I move to another device?

In Settings, choose Export backup. Keep the complete JSON file private; it is not encrypted by the app. On the other device, open Settings and import the backup. The app validates it and asks you to confirm before replacing all existing records. It does not merge histories. Export the destination’s records first if you want to keep them.

Can I use it offline?

Yes, after initial preparation in a supported browser. Open the app online, let offline preparation finish, then close and reopen it once online. Test a fresh opening without internet before relying on offline tracking. Installation is optional; this landing page itself is not available offline. Browser storage can be cleared or removed, so keep private backups.
